Today's Progress Report - Astro AI (Simple & Non-Technical)
Here is a simple, easy-to-understand list of all the features and updates we added to your app today.

What it does: Instead of having two separate, plain buttons for the camera and the gallery, we merged them into a single, beautiful upload card.

How it works: Tap the photo to open it in a beautiful, full-screen view. Tap the close button to return to the results page.
6. Smart Phone Back Button Support
What it does: Fixed the issue where pressing the back button on your phone while scanning would accidentally close the entire app.
How it works: Now, pressing back from a scanning screen will safely return you to the main Explore page.
7. Instagram-Style Exit Warning & Tab Shortcuts
What it does: Prevents the app from closing immediately when you accidentally press back on the main screen.
How it works:
If you are on the Numerology, Astro Map, Chats, or Profile pages and press back, the app automatically switches back to the main Today tab.
If you are on the Today tab and press back, a beautiful floating dark bubble appears saying "Press back again to exit". Pressing back twice within two seconds will close the app.

Why it is better: It now fills up the screen beautifully, matching the size and premium feel of the Astro Map natal chart.
10. Cleaner Explore Page
What it does: Removed the duplicate scan history section from the Explore tab.
Why it is better: It makes the Explore page shorter, cleaner, and easier to read, keeping your scan histories grouped inside their specific pages.
